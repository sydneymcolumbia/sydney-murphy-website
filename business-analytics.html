How I Work

I build measurement systems and the products on top of them. That means deciding what gets measured, how it is scored, and what the person on the other end sees, then owning the build until it ships.

Across three companies the pattern is the same: an uptime monitor that reads each service against how it normally behaves, an AI visibility score that reads engine answers for mentions and sources, and a wellness score that reads each test result against the user's own history. The analytical training comes from Carnegie Mellon, the instinct for the story behind the numbers from Columbia.

I write the technical specs the build runs on, set pricing and positioning from competitive analysis, and run the brand and content programme myself.
