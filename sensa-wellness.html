Sensa Wellness

Chief Marketing Officer & Head of Digital Products

From a test reading to a score you can act on.

Sensa Wellness makes a patented at-home saliva test that measures inflammation through CRP. Since 2025 I have led its digital product: the mobile app, the path from a raw test reading to the score a user sees, the website, and the UX, alongside the company's marketing.

The centrepiece is the relative wellness score. Instead of judging every result against one fixed cutoff, it reads each result against the user's own recent history, so the number reflects change in that person rather than distance from a population average. I designed the method and built it into the Flutter and Dart app.

Visit sensawellness.org
