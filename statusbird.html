StatusBird

Founder & Builder

Know before your customers do.

StatusBird is the SaaS company I founded and built. Every online store runs on a stack of third-party services: payments, shipping, email, ads, reviews. When one of them goes down, checkouts fail silently and revenue disappears while the store owner is the last to know.

StatusBird watches over 75 of the services e-commerce stores depend on, including Stripe, Shopify, Klaviyo, and the major shipping carriers, plus the store's own storefront. The moment a dependency breaks, it alerts the owner by SMS, email, Slack, Discord, or Teams, so they can act before customers ever notice.

What StatusBird Does

Monitors 75+ Services

Real-time monitoring across 16 categories: payments, e-commerce platforms, shipping, marketing, ads, tax, fulfillment, and more. Pre-built collections make setup one click.

Instant Multi-Channel Alerts

Smart, debounced alerts by email, SMS, Slack, Discord, and Teams, with correlation context when multiple services fail at once.

Own-Store Monitoring

Synthetic checks of your actual storefront catch outages that status pages miss, so you know your checkout is up even when nothing else reports a problem.

Reliability Intelligence

Public reliability grades, a downtime cost calculator, an SLA credit claim assistant, and an annual State of E-commerce Infrastructure report, all built on millions of status checks.

Live and in production
