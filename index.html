About

I'm a quantitative analyst and technical founder. I hold a Master of Science in Business Analytics from Carnegie Mellon's Tepper School of Business, with a Columbia Master's in data journalism behind it.

I've shipped two SaaS products: StatusBird, which watches the outside services an online store depends on and warns merchants when they start failing, and PositionBird, which measures how AI answer engines find and cite a merchant's content. I currently lead digital product at Sensa Wellness, a patented at-home diagnostics company, including the method behind its wellness score.

I work in Python, R, and SQL across predictive modelling, forecasting, and experiment design, and in Flutter and Dart on mobile.
