Data Journalism

Independent · 2021 – 2023

The story behind the numbers.

After my Master's in data journalism at Columbia, I reported and published data-driven health, technology, and international development stories for national and international outlets. The work meant sourcing, cleaning, and analysing public datasets in Python and R, including CDC public health data, doing the statistical work, and building the charts that carried the findings for a general audience.

It is the same discipline I now apply to products: find the measurement that matters, make it rigorous, and make it legible.
